Church Acts 2:42 Chronological Bible Reading Plan

Read the Bible in one year, in the order it happened.

From Creation to Revelation in 365 days. Job sits beside the patriarchs, the Psalms beside David’s life, the prophets beside the kings they spoke to, and the letters beside Paul’s journeys, so you read God’s story as one unfolding account.

“They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.” Acts 2:42

How it works

Each day has a reading of about 15 to 20 minutes. Tap a passage to open it online, then tick the box when you’ve read it. Your checkmarks are saved on this device only. Start any time: if you fall behind, just pick up where you left off.
