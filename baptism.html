Baptism

Baptism is a public profession of your faith in Jesus Christ, an outward sign of new life in Him. If you’ve put your trust in Jesus and haven’t yet been baptized, we’d love to celebrate with you.

Romans 6:4 · Matthew 28:19

Baby dedication

In a baby dedication, parents commit before God to raise their child to know and follow Jesus, and our church family promises to pray for and support them along the way.

1 Samuel 1:27–28
