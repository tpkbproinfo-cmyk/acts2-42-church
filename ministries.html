EXTRAordinary Kids

Extraordinary Love. Every Child. Every Ability.

Coming soon

A Christ-centered, safe, supportive and inclusive ministry where children of all abilities, including children with autism, developmental disabilities, sensory differences and other special needs, can learn about Jesus, build relationships, take part in church life and grow in faith at their own pace.

Our vision: a church where every child is welcomed, every family is supported, every ability is valued, and every child has an opportunity to experience God’s extraordinary love.

When & where 1st & 3rd Sundays of each month · 11:00 AM In our dedicated, sensory-friendly classroom

What makes EXTRAordinary Kids different

A place where every child belongs

Children are welcomed without judgment, and participation is adapted to each child’s communication style, sensory needs, abilities and comfort level.

A sensory-friendly environment

A quiet space, visual schedules, noise-reducing headphones, flexible seating and calming activities.

Faith taught in different ways

Picture cards, simple Bible stories, music, movement, repetition, hands-on activities and alternative communication methods.

Support for parents and caregivers

We partner with families, listen to their expertise about their children, and help parents worship with greater peace of mind.

We’re listening to families as we build this ministry. We’d love to hear what would help your child and family take part.
