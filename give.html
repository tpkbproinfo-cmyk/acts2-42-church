Give your talent

Discover your spiritual gift

“Having then gifts differing according to the grace that is given to us, let us use them.” Romans 12:6

Answer 14 quick statements honestly. You’ll see the gifts from Romans 12 that fit you best and the ministries where you could use them. It takes about 3 minutes.

There are no better answers here, only honest ones.

Choosing “Often” or “Almost always” will not give you a better score. Take your time, answer each statement truthfully, and let your results reflect how God has truly gifted you.

1. I quickly sense when something is spiritually right or wrong.
2. I notice practical needs and enjoy meeting them.
3. I enjoy studying the Bible and explaining it clearly to others.
4. People often come to me for advice or encouragement.
5. I find joy in giving generously to meet needs.
6. I enjoy organizing people and plans to reach a goal.
7. I’m drawn to people who are hurting or overlooked.
8. I feel compelled to speak up for God’s truth, even when it’s unpopular.
9. I’d rather work behind the scenes than be up front.
10. I like to research a topic until I understand it well.
11. I enjoy helping people take practical next steps in their faith.
12. I manage my money carefully so I can give more.
13. Others look to me to take charge when something needs to get done.
14. I can sit with people in their pain without needing to fix it.

Please answer every statement to see your results.

Where your gift goes

Every gift supports the work God has called us to:

  • Teaching and discipleship rooted in Scripture
  • Ministries for kids, youth and families
  • Equipping and training faithful leaders
  • Outreach and service in our community
  • Benevolence: caring for people in our church and community facing hardship
  • Missions: carrying the Gospel to our neighbors and the nations

Giving questions

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Once Church Acts 2:42 receives its 501(c)(3) designation, gifts will be tax-deductible.

How do I get a giving statement?

You’ll receive an automatic email receipt for every online gift. At the end of each fiscal year, we’ll also send you a notice with your giving statement.

Who can I talk to about giving?

Reach out to us at engage@acts2-42.church.
