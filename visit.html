Common questions

What should I wear?

Come as you are. There’s no dress code here. God meets us just as we are, and it’s His work, not ours, to wash us clean and clothe us in His righteousness (Isaiah 61:10).

Is there something for my children?

Yes. Every serving adult is background-checked and interviewed, at least two vetted adults are in every room, classrooms stay visible, and children are released only with a matching security tag.

Who may take Holy Communion?

Holy Communion is for the body of Christ, to remember what Jesus did for us on Calvary. Anyone who is a disciple of Jesus Christ is welcome to take part. We share it together on the first Sunday of each month. If you’re not yet a follower of Jesus, you’re welcome to sit quietly and pray, and our pastors would love to talk with you. “Do this in remembrance of Me.” (1 Corinthians 11:24)

What denomination are you?

We are seeking affiliation with The Christian and Missionary Alliance (C&MA), an evangelical Protestant denomination and global missions movement.

Is the building accessible?

Yes. Our building is wheelchair accessible. If you need any other help getting in or finding a seat, let our greeters know.

Let us know you’re coming

We’ll have someone ready to meet you and help you get settled.
