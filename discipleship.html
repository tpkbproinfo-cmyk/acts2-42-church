The Discipleship Pathway

From knowing the Word to making disciples who make disciples.

Seven steps, drawn from Acts 2:42, that help every believer grow from first steps in faith to multiplying their life in others.

The progression at a glance
LEARN → TRANSFORM → BELONG → PROCLAIM → MULTIPLY
01
The Word

Learn the Truth.

Biblical doctrine, interpretation, and application. Establish a solid foundation in Scripture.

Read the Bible in one year →
02
The Prayers

Depend on God.

Develop a life of personal and corporate prayer, worship, and spiritual devotion.

03
The Transformation

Become Like Christ.

Learn what it means to be a disciple through obedience, repentance, spiritual disciplines, and Christlike character.

Romans 12:1–2 · Luke 9:23
04
The Fellowship

Grow Together.

Build biblical community through unity, accountability, encouragement, and serving one another.

05
The Table

Remember Christ.

Explore communion, breaking bread, hospitality, and the shared life of the church.

06
The Witness

Share the Gospel.

Learn to articulate the Gospel, share your testimony, and live as a witness for Jesus Christ.

Acts 1:8 · Matthew 28:19–20
07
The Multiplication

Disciple Others.

Equip mature believers to mentor others, teach biblical truth, and reproduce disciples who make disciples.

2 Timothy 2:2

Ready for your next step?

Wherever you are on the pathway, we’d love to walk it with you. Join us Sundays at 11:00 AM.
