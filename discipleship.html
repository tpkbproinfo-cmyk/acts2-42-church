The Discipleship Pathway

From knowing the Word to making disciples who make disciples.

Seven steps, drawn from Acts 2:42, that help every believer grow from first steps in faith to multiplying their life in others.

The progression at a glance
LEARN → TRANSFORM → BELONG → PROCLAIM → MULTIPLY
01
The Word

Learn the Truth.

Biblical doctrine, interpretation, and application. Establish a solid foundation in Scripture.

All Scripture is inspired by God and is useful to teach us what is true and to make us realize what is wrong in our lives. It corrects us when we are wrong and teaches us to do what is right. God uses it to prepare and equip his people to do every good work.2 Timothy 3:16–17 (NLT) Read the Bible in one year →
02
The Prayers

Depend on God.

Develop a life of personal and corporate prayer, worship, and spiritual devotion.

Don’t worry about anything; instead, pray about everything. Tell God what you need, and thank him for all he has done. Then you will experience God’s peace, which exceeds anything we can understand. His peace will guard your hearts and minds as you live in Christ Jesus.Philippians 4:6–7 (NLT)
03
The Transformation

Become Like Christ.

Learn what it means to be a disciple through obedience, repentance, spiritual disciplines, and Christlike character.

Then he said to the crowd, “If any of you wants to be my follower, you must give up your own way, take up your cross daily, and follow me.”Luke 9:23 (NLT)
04
The Fellowship

Grow Together.

Build biblical community through unity, accountability, encouragement, and serving one another.

Two people are better off than one, for they can help each other succeed. If one person falls, the other can reach out and help. But someone who falls alone is in real trouble. Likewise, two people lying close together can keep each other warm. But how can one be warm alone? A person standing alone can be attacked and defeated, but two can stand back-to-back and conquer. Three are even better, for a triple-braided cord is not easily broken.Ecclesiastes 4:9–12 (NLT)
05
The Table

Remember Christ.

Explore communion, breaking bread, hospitality, and the shared life of the church.

For every time you eat this bread and drink this cup, you are announcing the Lord’s death until he comes again.1 Corinthians 11:26 (NLT)
06
The Witness

Share the Gospel.

Learn to articulate the Gospel, share your testimony, and live as a witness for Jesus Christ.

Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Teach these new disciples to obey all the commands I have given you. And be sure of this: I am with you always, even to the end of the age.Matthew 28:19–20 (NLT)
07
The Multiplication

Disciple Others.

Equip mature believers to mentor others, teach biblical truth, and reproduce disciples who make disciples.

You have heard me teach things that have been confirmed by many reliable witnesses. Now teach these truths to other trustworthy people who will be able to pass them on to others.2 Timothy 2:2 (NLT)
Our curriculum

Twelve studies, four stages: from first steps to the nations

We walk the pathway together in small groups, one study at a time. The core path (Survival Kit, MasterLife and Real-Life Discipleship) takes about 42 weeks. All twelve studies together take about 76 weeks, roughly a year and a half. Start at the beginning, or join where you are with a pastor’s guidance.

Stage 1 · Foundations

Build the habits of a growing believer

6–12 weeks · Start here

For new believers and anyone who wants a fresh start: daily time with God in prayer, Bible study and Scripture memory.

Pathway steps: The Word · The Prayers
  1. 1 Following JesusOptional first step 6 weeks · Choosing to Follow Jesus Meet Jesus through the stories of the Bible, told, discussed and retold together. Ideal for those who learn best by listening.
  2. 2 Survival Kit (Revised Edition)Core 6 weeks · 5 Keys to Spiritual Growth Build the daily habits of a growing believer, in a small group or one-on-one with a mentor.
Stage 2 · Growth

Become like Christ in every area of life

24 weeks, plus a 6-week companion

MasterLife is built around the Disciple’s Cross: time with the Master, the Word, prayer, fellowship, witness and ministry.

Pathway steps: The Transformation · The Fellowship · The Witness
  1. 3 MasterLife 1Core 6 weeks · The Disciple’s Cross The six core disciplines of a disciple, one week each, with daily devotions and Scripture memory.
  2. 4 MasterLife 2Core 6 weeks · The Disciple’s Personality Growing in Christlike character as the Spirit shapes who you are.
  3. 5 MasterLife 3Core 6 weeks · The Disciple’s Victory Standing firm in spiritual battles and living in the victory Christ has won.
  4. 6 MasterLife 4Core 6 weeks · The Disciple’s Mission Joining God on mission in your home, church and community.
  5. 7 Learning to SoarCompanion 6 weeks · How to Grow Through Transitions and Trials How God uses hardship to stretch our faith. A companion for anyone walking through a hard season.
Stage 3 · Multiplication

Make disciples who make disciples

12 weeks, plus 6 weeks of leader training

For maturing believers ready to walk alongside others and lead groups of their own.

Pathway steps: The Multiplication
  1. 8 Real-Life Discipleship Training ManualCore 12 weeks · Equipping Disciples Who Make Disciples Share, connect, minister and disciple: a simple, relational process for helping others grow.
  2. 9 Lead Like Jesus Study GuideFor leaders 6 weeks · Lessons from the Greatest Leadership Role Model of All Time Align heart, head, hands and habits so your leadership looks like Jesus’ servant love.
Stage 4 · Mission

Carry the Gospel to the nations

16 weeks · Acts 1:8

In step with the missionary vision of the Christian and Missionary Alliance: from Intercession City to the ends of the earth. First the why, then the how, then the who.

Pathway steps: The Witness · The Multiplication
  1. 10 The Biblical Basis of MissionsMission 6 weeks · God’s Heart for Every Nation The why: God’s heart for every nation, traced from Genesis to Revelation.
  2. 11 Truth That SticksMission 6 weeks · How to Communicate Velcro Truth in a Teflon World The how: share Bible truth through storying so it is remembered, lived and passed on.
  3. 12 Making Disciples Every Way People LearnMission 4 weeks · Teaching Truth That Connects The who: people learn in different ways, by reading, by hearing stories and by doing. Learn to adapt how you disciple so the Gospel connects with each one. Built on Making Disciples of Oral Learners.
